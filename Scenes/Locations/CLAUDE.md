# Locations, points of interest, girl views

## Data (`Scripts/Locations`, `Resources/Locations`)
- `LocationInfo`: `id` (`Ids.Location`), its scene, `unlocked`, `upgrade_level`, and `points_of_interest: Array[PoiInfo]`. It's available when it's unlocked **and** has a scene. The list and `starting_location` live in `locations_repo.tres`.
- `PoiInfo`: a spawn point (`id`: `Ids.Poi`, numbered and prefixed for its location, see `Scripts/Core/ids.gd`) with `required_upgrade_level` and `girl_setups`. **A girl can only appear at a POI where one of her `PoiGirlSetup`s is available** (its `conditions` pass).
- `PoiGirlSetup`: the per-girl, per-point config: `girl` (`Ids.Girl`), `conditions`, `pose` (`Ids.Pose`, which picks her scene from `GirlInfo.poses`), an optional one-off `scene` override, and `extra_interactions` (shown after the girl's own `GirlInfo.interactions`). A girl can have **several setups at one POI**: conditions are checked at placement time (context: girl, location, poi; no view), and one of the available ones is picked at random. Typical use: a `TimeCondition` setup for evenings plus a condition-free one.
- `GirlScheduler`: at every phase start `TimeManager` calls `schedule()`, which returns `Ids.Girl` → `GirlPlacement`. At most one girl per POI, chosen at random, and **every girl moves to a POI other than her previous one whenever possible** (it tries several random orders and keeps the best: most placed, then most moved). For new rules, subclass it and override `_pick_candidate()`.

## Scenes
- A location scene inherits `base_location.tscn` (`BaseLocation`) and contains `PointOfInterest` Marker2D nodes. **A marker's `poi` must match a `PoiInfo.id`**. `ContentValidator` reports mismatches in both directions at startup and in the smoke test. The marker's position and scale control how the girl looks there. `Scenes/base.gd` sets `location.info` before adding it to the tree.
- `BaseLocation` re-spawns girls from `WorldInfo.placements` whenever `GlobalSignals.girls_placed` fires.
- **Girl views are one scene per pose.** `Scenes/Girls/base_girl_location.tscn` (`BaseGirlLocation`) provides the Sprite, the `Select` Area2D (hover and click), `BubbleAnchor` and `MenuAnchor`. `Scenes/Girls/Poses/base_<pose>.tscn` inherits it and sets the frame layout (3 frames in one row) and the pose's looping animation (autoplay). `Scenes/Girls/<Girl>/<girl>_<pose>.tscn` inherits that and only sets her sheet `Assets/Characters/Girls/<Girl>/<Pose>.png`. `GirlInfo.poses` maps `Ids.Pose` to her scenes.
- Staged views (cutscenes, the action minigame) call `set_staged()`: not clickable, and in group `staged_girl_views` instead of `girl_views`.
- Location switching: the map emits `switch_location`, `base.gd` fades, swaps the scene, and emits `location_changed`.

## Recipes
- **New location:** add an `Ids.Location` value and its `Ids.Poi` values (`<location value>*100 + n`, key prefixed with the location key), create a scene inheriting `base_location.tscn` with markers, add a `LocationInfo` `.tres` with matching `PoiInfo`s, register it in `locations_repo.tres`, and add a map button plus an entry in `UI/Menu/map.gd` `location_buttons`. Removing one: delete its enum values, then fix everything the smoke test's validator check lists.
- **New girl:** add an `Ids.Girl` value (its lowercase key is her `.dlg` speaker and `Dialogs/<key>/` folder), create one sprite sheet per pose (3 frames in a row, same frame size as the others) and a `Scenes/Girls/<Girl>/<girl>_<pose>.tscn` per pose inheriting its `base_<pose>.tscn`, add a `GirlInfo` `.tres` (`poses` filled for every pose used, including each action scene's) (with an AtlasTexture `portrait`) registered in `girls_repo.tres`, create her interactions in `Resources/Interactions/<key>/` (copy another girl's folder, then fix the `id`s and dialog paths) and list the always-offered ones in `GirlInfo.interactions`, add `PoiGirlSetup`s where she can appear (with point-specific `extra_interactions`), and add `Dialogs/<key>/talk.dlg`.
